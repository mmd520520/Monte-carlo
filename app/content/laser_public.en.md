For laser products, the three numbers people ask about most are **what the device puts out**, **what the skin surface receives**, and **what is left at the target depth**. The first two can be calculated directly; the third needs simulation or measurement.

Below, the existing knee model (980 nm, from the original MATLAB model) is used for a trial calculation: enter power, spot and time to see the surface irradiance, the surface radiant exposure, and the radiant exposure at each depth below the skin.

**When reading the numbers**: the model has only soft tissue, patella and bone, with no skin or fat layer, and its optical properties are literature proxies. Use the results to compare **relative** sizes between settings; they are not patient doses and must not be used for external claims.
