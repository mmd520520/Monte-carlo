**Muse does not sell an "EEG headband"; it sells a loop**: put it on → hear your state in real time → see your progress → get coached. The hardware is only the entry point.

| Layer | What Muse does (public sources) |
|---|---|
| Sensing | Muse S Athena (2025): EEG, near-infrared (prefrontal oxygenation), heart rate and SpO₂, motion sensors |
| Metrics | Calm, Alpha Peak, Brain Recharge, Brain Age, sleep stages |
| Feedback | Sound like weather: quieter as you calm down, birdsong when you are on track; in near-infrared training an owl flies further the more you focus |
| Personalization | A personal baseline after a few sessions; weekly goals, practice plans; the Enso AI coach (subscription) |
| Sleep loop | Pink noise timed to deep sleep; smart wake-up; audio fades out once asleep |
| Evidence | Sleep staging agrees with clinical polysomnography 88–96% (Kappa 0.76); the site states it is "not a medical device" |
| Business | Devices $249.99–474.99; subscription ($49.99/year on the website) |
| User complaints | No data export; some features need a subscription; the band shifts overnight |

**How we differ**: Muse stops at "feedback to the person"; our headband adds a **light intervention**, so the loop becomes "measure the state → adjust the light → measure again". That step needs a scientific basis for the dose, and brings medical-device regulation with it.
