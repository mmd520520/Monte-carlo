**How much light actually reaches the brain?**\
Hair, skin tone and skull thickness each block some light, and we do not yet know how much this varies from person to person. Answering it needs a head model first, then this simulator to do the sums.

**Does the headband's own light disturb its own sensors?**\
The headband carries near-infrared and temperature sensors, and their readings may drift while the light is on. If so, light and measurement need to take turns, or use different wavelengths.

**How do we compare the "dose" of the two modes?**\
The Gamma and Alpha modes deliver light differently, and there is no common yardstick yet for "how much light each one gives". Agreeing on how to count the dose comes first; comparing effects comes after.
