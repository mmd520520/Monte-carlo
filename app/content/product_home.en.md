**Our product lines** (as publicly described on the company website):

| Product | Positioning | How simulation helps |
|---|---|---|
| Smart light-therapy headband | Wearable transcranial photobiomodulation; low-energy near-infrared, Gamma / Alpha modes; built-in EEG, heart-rate, SpO₂ and infrared temperature sensors; app with AI personalization | How much light reaches the cortex; how deep near-infrared sensing reaches; lookup tables for personalized dosing |
| Handheld high-intensity rehab laser | Musculoskeletal pain, joint rehabilitation, sports-injury recovery; high intensity, deep penetration, smart temperature control | Power, spot and time → dose at each depth; temperature rise |
| Optical skin sensing (planned) | — | How deep light travels in skin; which layer each probe distance is most sensitive to |

**Five layers from R&D to product** (taken apart from the Muse case):

1. **Sensing**: what is measured, and how accurately
2. **Metrics**: turning signals into meaningful numbers, backed by validation studies
3. **Feedback**: how the user feels it (sound, visuals, light)
4. **Personalization**: baselines, goals, coaching, automatic adjustment
5. **Evidence and business**: what can be claimed, how it is paid for
