**How does the user know the light is working?**\
Light itself is barely felt. Muse plays birdsong to tell you "you are calm right now"; we need feedback just as tangible, something that brings people back every day. Without it, even a good product feels like it does nothing.

**Which use first: sleep, focus or relaxation?**\
Doing all three at once spreads the effort thin. Pick one and make the whole chain work (measure, give feedback, adjust the light) before moving on to the next.

**Which evidence first?**\
It could be how much light gives how much effect, safety, or the user experience. The order decides what the product can claim and how it can be sold at launch.

**How should the future skin-sensing device be designed?**\
The key questions are how deep light travels in skin and how far apart to place the probes to read the layer we care about. This simulator already has a skin model, so it can answer part of this before any prototype exists.
