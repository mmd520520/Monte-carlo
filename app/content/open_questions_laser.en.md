**810, 980 or 1064 nm: which wavelength reaches deepest?**\
Computing three "depth versus light reaching it" curves in the same model ranks them relative to each other. The actual numbers still need measurement.

**Some studies use 20 W and others only 0.2 W. Can their results be compared?**\
With a hundredfold difference in power, comparing directly means nothing. Converting each to "how much light reaches the target depth" puts them on the same yardstick.

**How do our settings compare with similar devices on the market?**\
Running both sets of settings in the same model shows the difference at every depth.

**How warm does the skin get, and can it stay reliably within a safe range?**\
The simulation covers light, not heat. Answering this needs an added heating model, then bench measurements to confirm it.

The current knee model has no skin or fat layer, so all of the above are relative comparisons only: they do not replace measurement and cannot be used in marketing or product registration.
